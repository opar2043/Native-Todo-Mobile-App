# Native ToDo: Future Monetization & Feature Roadmap

This document outlines high-value, "value for money," and user-friendly features that can be added to Native ToDo. These features are designed to increase user retention, provide genuine utility, and create opportunities for premium (Pro) monetization, all while maintaining the clean, easy-to-use "Native Todo Aesthetic."

---

## 💎 Tier 1: High-Value Utility Features (Easy to Implement, High Impact)

These features provide immediate, tangible value to the user and make the app a daily necessity.

### 1. Home Screen Widgets (iOS & Android)
- **What it is:** Provide native widgets that sit on the user's phone home screen.
- **Value:** Users don't even have to open the app to get value. You can offer a "Next Prayer Countdown" widget, a "Today's Weather" widget, or a "Quick Add Task" widget.
- **Why it's premium:** Widgets keep your app top-of-mind and drastically increase daily active usage.

### 2. Smart Qibla Compass & AR Finder
- **What it is:** A beautiful compass in the Islamic Hub that uses the phone's magnetometer and GPS to point toward the Qibla.
- **Value:** Essential for travelers. Instead of a boring 2D compass, you could use the phone's camera for an AR (Augmented Reality) Qibla finder. 
- **Why it's premium:** High utility; users often pay for ad-free, accurate compass apps.

### 3. Habit Tracker & Gamification (Streaks)
- **What it is:** Allow users to track daily habits (e.g., "Read 2 pages of Quran", "Drink 2L Water", "Workout").
- **Value:** Visualizing "Streaks" (e.g., a 7-day flame icon) releases dopamine. If a user is on a 30-day streak, they will never delete your app.
- **Why it's premium:** You can offer basic task management for free, but restrict Habit Tracking to "Pro" members.

---

## 🧠 Tier 2: AI & Smart Integrations (The "Wow" Factor)

These features utilize modern technology to do the thinking for the user.

### 4. Context-Aware Task Scheduling (AI Assistant)
- **What it is:** The app looks at the user's tasks, the current weather, and the prayer times to suggest *what* they should do *when*.
- **Value:** E.g., If the weather is "Raining," the app suggests prioritizing indoor tasks. If Asr prayer is in 15 minutes, it suggests a quick 10-minute task rather than starting a 2-hour project.
- **Why it's premium:** "Smart Productivity" is a highly marketable buzzword. People pay for apps that organize their lives for them.

### 5. Quran Audio Sync & Bookmark
- **What it is:** When viewing a Surah, users can press "Play" to hear high-quality audio recitation that highlights the Arabic text word-by-word as it plays.
- **Value:** Incredible accessibility feature. Users can learn pronunciation easily.
- **Why it's premium:** Audio hosting costs money, making it a perfect feature to lock behind a one-time purchase or subscription.

---

## 🎨 Tier 3: Personalization & Health (Sticky Features)

### 6. Apple Health / Google Fit Integration
- **What it is:** Expand the Health Scan / BMI section to automatically pull in Daily Steps, Sleep Data, and Heart Rate from the user's native phone health app.
- **Value:** Turns your app into a complete dashboard for their body and soul.
- **Why it's premium:** Aggregating data into beautiful, easy-to-read charts is highly valued by users.

### 7. Premium Themes & Dark Mode Customizer
- **What it is:** The app currently has a stunning Coral/Light Gray theme. You can introduce a "Theme Store."
- **Value:** Users love personalization. Let them unlock beautiful, curated themes like "Midnight Obsidian" (OLED Dark Mode), "Desert Gold", or "Oceanic Blue."
- **Why it's premium:** Aesthetic customization is the #1 easiest way to monetize an app (similar to Telegram or Discord Nitro).

### 8. Offline Mode & Cloud Sync (Pro Feature)
- **What it is:** The app works entirely offline using local storage (SQLite/AsyncStorage) and syncs instantly to Firebase the second the internet connects.
- **Value:** Users can add tasks or read the Quran on airplanes, in subways, or in remote areas.
- **Why it's premium:** Uninterrupted offline access is a gold standard for professional utility apps.

---

## 📈 Suggested Monetization Strategy ("Freemium" Model)

To make this app "Value for Money", offer a highly generous free tier and a compelling "Pro" tier:

* **Free Tier:** Basic Todo List, Standard Calculator, Weather conditions, Text-only Quran, Standard Prayer Times.
* **Native Pro ($2.99/mo or $29.99 Lifetime):** 
  - Unlocks Home Screen Widgets
  - Unlocks AI Task Suggestions
  - Unlocks Premium Themes (Dark Mode)
  - Unlocks Audio Quran Recitation
  - Ad-Free experience (if you choose to run ads on the free tier)
